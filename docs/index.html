RESEARCH SCOPE

This is computational feasibility research. CT anatomy is a proxy for potential cartilage stock. A verified geometric fit does not establish safe bending, machinability, tissue durability or clinical readiness.

01 / HELIX STRIP PLACEMENT

Native curvature as a starting configuration

A selected adult eighth-cartilage example accommodates the 1.5 and 2.4 mm helix-strip options at the existing 0.8 mm margin. Its bending simulation then asks how that cut could become an ear rim.

A favorable development example, not a population success rate. Section depth: 2.7 mm. Mechanical parameters remain conditional.

03 / CONNECTED CARTILAGE

Some cuts span adjacent labels.

The whole-release junction search found verified multi-label cuts in four primary case identifiers. A previously harvested, age-unverified G4 case supported three of four components.

That result is separate from the ten-adult cohort. The native gaps remain; no artificial tissue bridges were added.

04 / MODELED SHAPE RETENTION

Guide support and suture retention

In the illustrative antihelix simulation, shape error changes from 0.27 mm under a guide to 0.47 mm with sutures alone. Stitches pull; unlike a guide, they cannot push.

The animated starting cut is unverified in its source cartilage. This demonstrates conditional forming behavior, not an accepted reconstruction.
