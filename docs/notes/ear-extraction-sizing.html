Intact ear extraction and population-reference sizing

Correction to the earlier preview

The original OnlyEar_IdealReference.stl contains two disconnected surface bodies. Splitting with repair disabled reveals an intact, watertight ear with 63,204 triangles and an open surrounding-surface body with 41,509 triangles. The original file contains 104,713 triangles in total.

The earlier display cropped faces according to a manually drawn projected outline. That crop removed valid ear faces, including portions of the lobule, and retained some surrounding surface. Its missing faces were not a feature of the intact ear. The original whole file's open boundaries must not be attributed to its closed ear component.

The revised importer extracts the unique closed connected component. All 63,204 original ear triangles are preserved; zero ear faces are deleted, zero faces are added, and no hole filling or smoothing occurs. Full-resolution geometry is also used for the preview. Region colors assign triangles without deleting any. The surrounding body can be shown separately for context.

This establishes one connected, consistently wound, positive-volume, topologically watertight external-ear surface with zero boundary edges. It does not establish the absence of all self-intersections or provide native cartilage segmentation. Skin and soft lobule remain part of the external reference.

The source ear has Euler characteristic −2, corresponding to two through-openings in this connected orientable surface. These openings are bounded by existing tissue surfaces; they are not missing mesh faces or open boundary edges. They are preserved and flagged for anatomical review, rather than filled with guessed tissue.

Source: Díez-Montiel et al., 2024 supplementary models, CC BY-SA 4.0. Original archive and member bytes are preserved; geometry selection, transformation and attribution are recorded in the generated report.

Literature used to choose an adult benchmark

Bozkir et al. (2006), Aesthetic Plastic Surgery measured 341 healthy adults aged 18–25: 150 women and 191 men. Female mean ear heights were 59.7 mm left and 59.5 mm right; male means were 63.1 and 62.9 mm. Female mean widths were 31.3 and 31.2 mm; male widths were 33.3 and 33.1 mm. Averaging sides yields height means of 59.6 and 63.0 mm. Weighting these rounded means by sample size gives (59.6 × 150 + 63.0 × 191) / 341 = 61.5044 mm. We chose 61.5 mm as a cohort-based adult reference, not a universal normal or clinical threshold.

Sforza et al. (2009), Forensic Science International examined 843 healthy participants aged 4–73 and found size differences by age and sex. This supports retaining population and age context rather than assigning one “correct” size to every ear.

Japatti et al. (2018), Annals of Maxillofacial Surgery studied 505 Maharashtrian adults using photographs. Its measurement definition uses tangents at the upper helix and lower lobule, and the study emphasizes population variation. We use the full intact surface's tangent-plane span along a documented superior axis as an explicit computational approximation. That axis still needs anatomical review; it is not a recovered Frankfurt-plane registration.

How the model is rescaled

The old manually picked landmark gap is replaced by the extrema of the intact ear along the previously documented superior axis. Parallel tangent planes span the entire ear including lobule. A single uniform scale sets that span to 61.5 mm; rotations are proper and preserve handedness.

The resulting ear dimensions along its local axes are approximately 35.79 × 61.50 × 26.42 mm. Width and depth follow the source's proportions; they are not independently forced to population means. A height-normalized individual shape is not a statistically average ear.

The STL's original physical units remain unconfirmed. Rescaling creates a declared adult reference in millimeters, not recovery of a verified source-patient size. Calibrated scans or physical measurements of the intended target, surgical component boundaries and reviewed depths remain necessary for patient-specific work.

The component footprints are rebuilt at the new scale, while their engineering thicknesses and helix-strip width remain fixed. Containment searches are rerun with rigid poses. Old fit certificates are not inherited by resized pieces. The original outputs remain under their original size definitions.

Verification and outputs

Tests check that every extracted triangle exists in the original source, no faces are invented, the display preserves all ear faces, closure is retained, the full height is exactly the target, and transverse proportions are unchanged. The closed PLY and STL exports are supplied with the revised Component Lab. The original public archive remains unchanged.

Rebuild with scripts/rebuild_population_reference.py, then scripts/rebuild_population_pool.py. Audit exported component placements with scripts/verify_components.py and the pooled run with scripts/verify_pool.py. The computational certificates apply to draft component stock, not to the external skin surface as an implant.
