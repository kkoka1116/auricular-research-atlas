Delivered: source-derived component development iteration

A checksum-verified surgical ear reference now supports an explicit 60 mm benchmark and editable projected component outlines. These are planar engineering components based on a real external reference, with unreviewed depths and attachments. They are a reviewable development milestone, not completion of the surgeon-reviewed reference requirement. See docs/research/component-methods.md.
