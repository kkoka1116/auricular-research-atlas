Interpretation and next design question

These results support expanding the geometric inventory and testing native connections. They do not establish that an intact surgical junction consists entirely of cartilage, or that an older cadaveric graft has suitable mechanical properties. CCSeg supplies patient CT anatomy, not tissue-bank specimens; rib/side names remain provisional.

The current contoured base follows an external-ear surface and may demand excessive carving depth. Literature-supported alternatives include a shallower support, an antihelix carved into the base, or separate base sections joined with fixation. Their dimensions and assembly constraints require explicit design and surgical review. The current bounded search cannot prove that reconstruction or a different placement is impossible.
