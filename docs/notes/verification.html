Independent artifact checks

scripts/verify_demo.py checked all 41 model-file hashes. For every feasible method–blank result, an independent ray-based containment path tested all framework vertices and triangle centroids: 5,378 points per placement, across five successful placements. Direct closest-surface distances at those points exceeded the required 0.8 mm boundary margin.

These spot checks supplement the full convex half-space certificate; they do not replace it. The independent check also integrated the reference's top height field and matched its closed-mesh volume. Rounded JSON geometry was used in this audit; unit tests separately check the generator's full-precision geometry.

The machine-readable results are in output/demo/verification.json.

The optimizer found verified placements for 3/5 synthetic configurations; each baseline fit 1/5. These deliberately constructed software cases do not estimate an anatomical donor-match rate. Failure to find a placement is not proof of impossibility. Negative clearance is a supporting-plane violation, not Euclidean outside distance.

Local-displacement amplitudes of 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0 mm produced symmetric surface RMSE values of approximately 0.067, 0.134 and 0.267 mm after rigid alignment. This demonstrates an increasing response in those cases, not validated clinical defect detection. Noise and missing surfaces also create apparent errors, and remain separately labelled.
