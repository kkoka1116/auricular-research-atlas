CCSeg development methods

Data and identity

Configuration: config/ccseg.json. Repository haifan-gong/CCSeg, commit 9c288ea4585c820de202ebd7838f13072f267f1e. The first three lexicographically sorted training-mask paths were selected before inspecting anatomy: T108-09M, T109-18F and T113-08M. One matching CT (T108-09M) supplies context. All cases remain development data. No independent-person inference, demographic analysis or pilot confidence interval is made.

The importer requires explicit millimetre units, a coded affine, agreement between qform and sform when both exist, finite data and an orthogonal voxel grid. It rejects shear rather than silently using an incorrect Euclidean distance transform. Label data must be nonnegative integers. The prototype expects the inspected label set 0–20 and stops if spatial ordering is inconsistent with its provisional mapping. Original files are retained; compressed-file SHA256, physical affine, spacing, shape and orientation are reported. RAS means positive X right, Y anterior, Z superior. For the front rendering only, coordinates become (-X,Z,Y), placing patient right on screen left. Exported PLY files remain in original physical RAS millimetres.

All raw labels remain authoritative. Candidate L1–L10 correspond to integers 1–10 and candidate R1–R10 to 11–20. This is an explicit spatial inference, not a verified author label dictionary. It must not silently become a clinical annotation. Anatomical naming, landmark measurements and surgery-specific usable regions require review.

Geometry

No smoothing, morphological closing, hole filling, cartilage thickening or convexification is performed. Full-resolution 0.5-level marching cubes is exported per label, with handedness correction when needed. Coarsened step-3 marching cubes supplies display meshes only. Neither surface determines matching feasibility. The domain is the union of the native occupied voxel cuboids. Voxel-count volume and extracted-mesh volume are both recorded and need not be identical. Voxel-center PCA spans are descriptive dimensions, not curved clinical cartilage lengths. Six-connected component counts expose fragments; they do not diagnose anatomical discontinuity.

Labels at an acquisition edge are flagged using the original scan extent. Padding closes only the computational boundary; it cannot establish that the tissue was fully scanned. The matching scope is the observed segmentation. No independent estimate of segmentation accuracy is available here.

Placement and the complete-solid certificate

The unchanged procedural reference has 60 mm height and no surgical approval. Its column thickness is known from construction, rather than measured as arbitrary wall-normal thickness. The configured minimum column thickness is checked before screening. Scale is fixed at one. An excluded-region mask has not been supplied; all unselected or unsegmented voxels are unavailable.

For each side, candidate label unions 6+7, individual 8 and individual 9 are screened. Disconnected pieces are never bridged. Their joint union is only a mathematical search domain, not a claim that it is a physically harvestable blank.

Baselines center the framework at the domain's voxel centroid, either in a fixed anatomical orientation or using PCA axes sorted to align its long and short dimensions. The optimized method retains these starts, locally refines them with bounded Powell search, and includes a seeded differential-evolution candidate. Its search objective penalizes sampled clearance deficits using an interpolated distance field. This objective guides search only; it does not certify feasibility. Search budget and seed are retained. Optimization may miss feasible poses.

For independent verification from the search surrogate:

  • The height-field framework is partitioned into triangular prisms and then tetrahedra, covering its entire solid. The sum of tetrahedral volumes is tested against the framework's integrated/mesh volume. The verifier rejects scaling and reflection.
  • Let h be half the physical voxel diagonal and D(c) the Euclidean distance from a foreground voxel center c to the nearest background voxel center, computed on an explicitly background-padded orthogonal grid. For a point p in that cell, D(c) - ||p-c|| - h is a conservative lower bound on its distance to the background cuboid union. Background/outside points never receive positive clearance.
  • A tetrahedron is contained in the ball centered at its centroid with radius equal to its most distant vertex. Subtracting this radius from the centroid's lower clearance gives a bound valid throughout the tetrahedron, by the triangle inequality.
  • Tetrahedra whose bounds exceed the prescribed margin plus 1e-6 mm pass. Ambiguous tetrahedra are bisected along their longest edge. Failure to certify a centroid or reaching the subdivision limit returns not certified. Only complete coverage permits verified feasible.

This deliberately conservative certificate can reject valid poses. It prevents a surface-only test from missing cavities or gaps traversed by the interior. Tests include a central void despite all external vertices being inside, separated regions, anisotropic spacing, coordinate reflection and comparison with exact distances to background boxes. The certificate applies to the segmented voxel solid, not automatically to biological tissue or to the smoothed visual interpretation of a CT.

Independent positive control and sensitivity

For each candidate 6+7 union, place a 2 × 2 × 2 mm software cube at the deepest labeled voxel center. It has its own exact tetrahedral partition. An independent audit computes the Euclidean gap between its axis-aligned box and every background voxel box; positive certificates must agree with this check. This is a calibration object, not an approved reconstruction component. It prevents an all-negative full-framework screen from being mistaken for proof that the checker cannot accept any placement.

Additional 0.5 and 1.0 mm inward margins are applied at the same poses. This is a boundary-uncertainty scenario, without reoptimization, rather than a measured segmentation error model. The report does not infer biological thickness, calcification, tool accessibility or material performance.

Outputs and reproducibility

auricular-lab anatomy produces an offline HTML report, numerical JSON, paired-method CSV, 60 unsmoothed PLY meshes and 25 axial context PNGs. The CT planes use a fixed 0–350 native-value display window; physical grid agreement is checked, but independent HU calibration and tissue classification are not claimed. Keep the report's sibling folders for links and CT planes to work. The data and derivatives remain local while the contradictory repository license statements are clarified.

scripts/verify_ccseg.py checks input/output hashes, all label inventories and coordinate checks, the placement matrix, fixed-scale transforms and independent positive-control results. An optional second report is compared using scientific fingerprints, which exclude runtime and execution metadata. Unit tests exercise the certificate independently of the CT sample. Full framework failures remain results; they do not establish impossibility or invalidate modular reconstruction.
