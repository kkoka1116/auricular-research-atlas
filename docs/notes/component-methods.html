Published ear reference and component allocation: development iteration 1

17 September 2026. This milestone supplies a reviewable source-derived draft and a reproducible component allocation experiment. It does not supply a surgeon-approved auricular framework.

Reference, units and annotations

The original Díez-Montiel et al. supplement is preserved in data/public_anatomical/Microtia2024/SuppContent_3DModels.zip. Its published MD5 matches; SHA-256 is e8abf22e163af9c94cb3192ee31617275d79d9274e6e135e32cded3d93e069f7. The record declares CC BY-SA 4.0. The report retains attribution and modification notices. CCSeg derivatives remain local because its pinned README has conflicting license statements.

OnlyEar_IdealReference.stl contains an external surface, some surrounding head surface and a lobule; it has 104,713 triangles and is not closed. It is not a native cartilage segmentation. No separately named cartilage-component meshes were supplied in the archive.

STL does not encode physical units. We do not claim the original coordinates are verified millimetres. Two manually drafted source-XZ landmarks, (-124, 6) and (-101, -46), define a projected long axis. A proper rotation and uniform scale set their projected separation to a chosen 60 mm benchmark; the recorded factor is 1.0552330820 mm per source unit. A translation centers the depth for viewing. This is an explicit target-size convention, not recovery of recipient anatomy. The transformation is applied once, before matching.

Projected auricle and lobule outlines distinguish viewing regions without filling the source surface. They are new engineering annotations, not author-provided labels. Quantitative source geometry is preserved; 0.7 mm vertex clustering is used only for display.

Draft framework and editability

config/components.json defines source-plane controls for a base, helix ribbon, branched antihelix and tragus-region piece. The outlines were drafted after visually inspecting the published surface. They are extruded into closed solids using explicit thicknesses (2.4, 3.0, 3.0 and 3.4 mm). These values are engineering assumptions, not inferred cartilage thicknesses or surgical recommendations.

The support plate excludes the soft lobule and has a different footprint from the former 60 mm whole-ear relief solid. The draft does not yet reproduce full 3D cartilage contours, validated attachment geometry, antitragus detail or additional components a surgeon may require. Assembly offsets illustrate stacking; they do not validate mechanical support or joinability.

The browser supports outline-point dragging, thickness/offset edits, rim-width edits and explicit benchmark sizing. It exports a configuration and individual draft STL files. Saved allocation results remain tied to the evaluated configuration; edits display a pending-draft warning. Rebuild with the exported configuration to obtain new results. An unedited and an edited configuration were compared between JavaScript and Python: identical connectivity and maximum vertex difference 2.7e-14 mm across four components in each configuration.

Two distinct stock questions

  1. Rigid assembled-shape components: Each modeled part must fit in its final draft shape through rotation and translation alone.
  2. Straight helix stock hypothesis: The helix is replaced during stock matching by an 84.21 × 3.19 × 3.00 mm strip. Projected centerline length is retained and width is chosen to preserve modeled volume exactly. It is displayed alongside the curved assembly target. Equal volume and length do not establish bending mechanics, strain limits, warping or viable manufacture. A successful strip placement would therefore remain conditional on unvalidated reshaping.

No splicing, automatic subdivision, scaling during matching, or inter-case stock pooling is allowed in either experiment.

Search, assignment and verification

Each of the three original development scans is tested on each side, for six side trials. Candidate stock is the original label region proposed as rib 5, the union of proposed ribs 6+7, rib 8 and rib 9. Provisional labels are offset by ten for the second side. The anatomy report's mask hashes and spatial-order checks are verified. An official numeric dictionary and anatomical review are still needed.

The native labeled voxel cuboids define stock. Gaps, holes and disconnected regions remain unavailable; the 6+7 union does not imply a surgically usable synchondrosis. Exclusions, calcification and tissue viability were not annotated.

A 0.8 mm boundary margin and 0.8 mm inter-piece spacing are prespecified engineering assumptions. Fixed anatomical and PCA bounding-box orientations provide reproducible baselines. Seeded differential evolution and Powell refinements propose additional candidates, using a distance-field surrogate. The optimized allocation pool includes baseline candidates. All component dimensions remain fixed.

A separate verifier checks the whole solid, not just vertices. Exact polygon-prism tetrahedra are covered by conservative native-voxel clearance bounds with adaptive subdivision. Simple-polygon validation prevents self-intersecting outlines; analytic area-times-depth, mesh volume and tetrahedral volume agree. A failure to certify is not proof of geometric impossibility.

Joint allocation maximizes the number of certified pieces, then summed certified clearance. This is not a clinical utility score. For different nonoverlapping label sets, two certified inward margins establish separation. For shared stock, separating axes of enclosing oriented boxes conservatively prove spacing. Failed box separation is inconclusive and may reject nonintersecting detailed shapes. Thus multiple components may share a cartilage region, but overlapping allocations cannot pass the joint check.

Selected placements are re-certified, and independent exact distances to nearby background voxel boxes are calculated at vertices and tetrahedral centroids. These spot checks supplement the full-solid certificate. PLY export coordinates are checked against saved transforms with float32-appropriate tolerance.

Observed results and their limits

There are 120 component-region search problems (five stock shapes × four regions × six side trials) and 1,193 retained candidate poses. Both baselines allocate zero pieces in this development iteration. Optimized search certifies the tragus-region piece in candidate L6+7 and R6+7 of T109-18F. The other two scans have no allocated pieces under these settings. Both architecture options reuse the same two accepted tragus placements; these are not four distinct successes.

The two clearance lower bounds are approximately 0.804 and 0.801 mm. Independent sample minima are approximately 1.91 and 1.59 mm. No complete four-piece stock allocation was certified. All failed and partial attempts are retained. The viewer can display an unallocated candidate in red, without adding it to the certified assembly.

These results test the stated draft shapes, margins, search and verifier. They do not evaluate whether an experienced surgeon can reconstruct an ear from these cartilages. In particular, planar draft outlines, technique-dependent carving/reshaping, incomplete junction information and conservative bounds limit that interpretation. Filename suffixes have not been used as verified demographics.

A second clean output directory reran all searches with the same seed and inputs and reproduced the same scientific fingerprint: e3134b5a750d33697527162731b35afd035566900b509bfd9d473a9445b0e37a. Six side trials are repeated observations from three scans, not six independent participants. No inferential efficacy claim is appropriate.

Reproduce

.venv/bin/python scripts/download_ear_reference.py
.venv/bin/python -m auricular_lab.cli components --output output/components
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q
.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_components.py output/components
.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_editor.py output/components

# Independent search rerun, without copying caches:
.venv/bin/python -m auricular_lab.cli components --output output/components-reproduced
.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_components.py output/components --compare output/components-reproduced

The local public source archive and pinned CCSeg files are required. For an edited design, save the downloaded configuration as config/components-review.json and pass --config config/components-review.json --output output/components-review. Python validates outlines again before accepting them for analysis.

The next scientific gate is review of the actual component boundaries, depth profiles, attachment requirements, cartilage identities and permitted reshaping. Revised constraints form a new development iteration; the locked independent pilot remains a later phase of the original plan.
